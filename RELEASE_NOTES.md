**Fretboard Lab Community Edition is free to download and use.**

Fretboard Lab 0.2.0 rebuilds the fretboard workspace for chord choice, personal fingerings and progression playback.

- Choose chord root, color and bass with fixed controls, including chords outside the key. Standard menus and separate Settings and Activation dialogs keep the workspace clear.
- Explore degrees, richer chord colors and open strings that follow tuning and capo. Duplicate generated fingerings keep the valid variant with the most sounding strings; personal fingerings support finger, thumb and partial-barre annotations.
- Improved guitar audition supports per-string attacks, holds and stops. One bottom transport selects strum, arpeggio or together and plays the current chord, a harmony candidate, a chosen step or the progression.
- Projects in formats 1 and 2 open without rewriting the original; saving uses format 3. Installer availability is confirmed separately after verification for each platform.
